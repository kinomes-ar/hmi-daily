Buttons come back at Volvo and VW, China stretches the HUD to 43 inches, and a notepad on the back of a phone takes the week's hearts

Five editions, 80 stories, 15 hearts and 9 skips. The week's arc was reduction: Volvo's Thomas Ingenlath promised tactile controls, each with its own shape; the ID.3 GTI put a button strip back under its 12.9-inch screen; VW's Mission Efficiency dropped the centre screen for a phone on a rail. Cockpit

Cabins argued about how much screen is enough. Volvo confirmed physical controls, each with a unique shape, previewed by a spring 2027 show car; the ID.3 GTI restored a climate button strip and a volume knob; VW's Mission Efficiency has no centre screen at all, only a phone rail and an e-paper climate strip. Chinese launches went the other way: Li Auto's i9 with a 29-inch 6K screen and seats that turn, BYD's Formula S with a 26-inch AR-HUD and bolt-on rails, Geely's Zhanjian 700 with five screens and a fridge, Voyah's Dream 9 on HarmonySpace 6. Kia's PV7 van carried a 14.6-inch display.

座舱在争论“多少屏幕才够”。沃尔沃确认实体按键回归，每个都有独特形状，2027年春的展车先行预告；ID.3 GTI恢复了空调按键条和音量旋钮；大众Mission Efficiency干脆没有中控屏，只有手机导轨和一条电子纸空调条。中国新车走向另一端：理想i9的29英寸6K屏与可旋转座椅、比亚迪Formula S的26英寸AR-HUD与可拆装导轨、吉利战舰700的五块屏和冰箱、岚图梦想家9的鸿蒙座舱6。起亚PV7厢式车配14.6英寸屏。
